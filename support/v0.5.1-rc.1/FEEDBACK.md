# Feedback and bug reports — Juno v0.5.1 Beta

Version `0.5.1-rc.1` · Windows 11 x64 · Unsigned installer

Juno v0.5.1 Beta is public but still a beta. Specific, reproducible reports are more useful than
a score.

**Support email:** `madebytanishk@gmail.com`

## Primary route: from inside Juno

1. Open **Settings → Help & feedback**.
2. Choose **Report a bug** or **Share feedback**.
3. Describe what happened. Add steps, expected behavior and actual behavior.
4. Turn on **Include technical diagnostics** only if you are comfortable sharing the allowlisted
   fields it lists.
5. Review the report preview; it is the exact text that will be used.
6. Choose **Open Email Draft**, review the message, and press **Send** yourself.

Juno does not send the report, has no reporting server and cannot confirm that it arrived.

## Alternative: GitHub Issues

Open an issue in this public repository:

<https://github.com/tanishkfr/juno-beta/issues/new/choose>

The bug form asks for the version, Windows build, affected area, expected and actual behavior,
reproduction steps, repeatability and optional sanitized evidence. Anything you paste is public.
Do not post logs, keys, private documents or personal data.

## Security problems

Do not open a public issue with exploit details. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or email
`madebytanishk@gmail.com` and mark the report security-sensitive. Never include live credentials
or private data.

## Upgrade reports

If you are upgrading from v0.5.0, include the old and new versions, whether companions and
conversations remained visible, and whether your provider still shows as connected. Do not include
conversation content unless it is necessary and safe to share.
